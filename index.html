House rules

  • Pick one team to win each gameweek. Picks close at the first kick-off of the round, and you can change yours until then.
  • Win and you're through. A draw or a defeat and you're out.
  • Every team once only. Use a team and it's gone for the rest of the game.
  • Forget to pick and you're out.
  • Everyone left goes out in the same round? Then they all survive to the next one.
  • Your match gets postponed? You survive that round, and the team still counts as used.
  • Last one standing takes the pot. No rebuys.
